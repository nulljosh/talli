Open data

The pay dates and rate tables Talli runs on, as plain JSON. Free to use, no key, works from any website. Built for advocates, caseworkers and anyone making a tool for people on disability assistance.

Every figure links to its official source. Rates change, so check the source before you rely on a number. Updated 2026-10-02.

Endpoints

GET /api/open/pay-dates

BC income and disability assistance cheque issue dates.

GET /api/open/rates

BC support and shelter tables by family size, earnings limits, supplements, and federal amounts (Canada Disability Benefit, Canada Child Benefit, RDSP).

GET /api/open/provinces

British Columbia, Alberta and Ontario programs: what they pay and how earnings are treated.

GET /api/open/all

All of it in one file.

Use it

curl https://talli.heyitsmejosh.com/api/open/pay-dates
fetch('https://talli.heyitsmejosh.com/api/open/rates').then(r => r.json())

Limit: 60 requests a minute per address. Responses are cached for an hour. Want another province added? It is one small data file in data/provinces. Send a pull request.
