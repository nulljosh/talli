Show HN: Talli – Disability tax credit, RDSP and CDB, sorted

Canada offers real money to disabled people and their families, and almost none of it is claimed, because the paperwork is a maze. The DTC unlocks the RDSP, the RDSP unlocks grants and bonds, the CDB stacks on top, and every form assumes you already know the others exist. Talli is the map. It tells you what you qualify for, in what order, and what each step is worth. Web at talli.heyitsmejosh.com, source on GitHub.
