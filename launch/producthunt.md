## First comment

Hi, I'm Josh, I built Talli.

Canada offers real money to disabled people and their families, and almost none of it is claimed, because the paperwork is a maze. The DTC unlocks the RDSP, the RDSP unlocks grants and bonds, the CDB stacks on top, and every form assumes you already know the others exist. Talli is the map. It tells you what you qualify for, in what order, and what each step is worth.

Free. Web accounts can upgrade for document storage. Happy to answer anything.
